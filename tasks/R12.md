# R12 · Design as decisions

## Goal

The Spec's Design tab lists decision topics with options proposed by the assistant. Each choice is a decision record.

## Scenarios that prove it

- `features/design-decisions.feature` (all scenarios)

## Data

- Stored with the spec (front matter `decisions:` or `.keel/spec/decisions.json`, whichever fits the store best):
  `{ topic, title, origin: 'template' | 'assistant', reason_added?, options: [{ id, name, summary, pro, con, serves: [REQ], fit: { kind: 'follows' | 'breaks' | 'conflicts' | 'adds-concern', text }, recommended }], chosen?, own?, reason?, record? }`.
- Decision records in `docs/decisions/NNNN-<slug>.md` from the decision-record template, with "Alternatives considered" listing the options not chosen.

## Files that are likely to change

- Spec template (`seed/templates/spec-standard.md`): a `decision_topics` list (form, stack, storage, model access, testing). New template version.
- `src/lib/generate/spec.ts`: replace Design prose with `proposeDecisions(intent, standards, repoFacts, template)`. One model call returns topics and options, validated with zod. Extra topics must name the requirement that needs them.
- New `src/lib/repo/facts.ts`: read facts from the folder (package.json dependencies, lock files, language files) so "Keep current" can be offered. No network.
- New `src/lib/decisions/records.ts`: `decide(topic, option, reason)` writes or rewrites the record while the spec is a draft; after approval it creates a change request and a superseding record. Uses the existing ADR numbering.
- `src/lib/decisions/own.ts`: review a user-written option against requirements and standards (model call), returning a fit line.
- `src/lib/generate/approach.ts`: assemble Approach and Components from the chosen options; recompute feasibility notes and flagged concerns that depend on them.
- `src/lib/gates/*`: plan gate adds "every topic decided".
- `src/app/(project)/spec/page.tsx`: Design tab as in the mockup: progress line, a card per topic (options, own option, reason, Decide, Change, Cancel), Approach summary, Standards applied.

## Notes

- Options must be concrete and comparable; no more than three proposed per topic.
- The assistant never changes a decision on its own. A new requirement may make it add a comment on a topic ("REQ-014 needs real-time updates; your read-back choice polls every minute").

## As built

- **Template:** `seed/templates/spec-standard-v3.md` is `requirements-and-design-spec@3`. Its `decision_topics` are Form, Language and framework, Storage, Model access and Testing, and it adds a Design section. Specs from templates @1 and @2 have no topics, and the plan gate does not wait for them.
- **Data:** `.keel/design.json` (`src/lib/decisions/design.ts`) holds the topics with their options, the choice, your own option, the reason and the record path. It also holds a choice waiting for a change request (`pending`). The file is committed with each change. `undecided(root)` feeds the plan gate.
- **Proposals:** `src/lib/generate/design.ts` makes one model call, validated with zod. Each topic gets at most three options and exactly one recommended. A topic the assistant adds is kept only if `reason_added` names a REQ. "Keep current: <stack>" comes first for Language and framework when `src/lib/repo/facts.ts` finds a manifest. Without a model, the topics come back with no options and you write your own. On regenerate, decided topics are kept; only undecided ones are proposed again.
- **Deciding:** `decide()` in `src/lib/decisions/records.ts` works like this:
  - Draft spec: it writes `docs/decisions/NNNN-<topic>.md` with the existing ADR numbering, listing "Alternatives considered". A change rewrites the same record and adds "Changed from X on date" under `## Changes`.
  - Approved spec: the choice is held and raised as a significant change request. Approving it writes a superseding record (`supersedes:`); the old record is closed with `superseded_by:`. Rejecting it drops the choice.
  - Your own option gets a fit line from the model (`own.ts`).
  - The spec's Design and Approach sections are rebuilt from the choices, and a chosen option that breaks a standard, conflicts or adds a concern is added to Flagged concerns.
- **Gate:** the plan also needs every topic decided. The reason names the undecided ones: "Needs every design topic decided (Model access)".
- **UI:** the Design card on the Spec page (`design-panel.tsx`, `design-actions.ts`) shows:
  - progress ("N of M topics decided");
  - a card per topic with the options (summary, pro and con, requirements served, fit line, Recommended), your own option, the reason, Decide, and Change/Cancel for a decided topic;
  - the Approach assembled from the choices.

  It is a section of the page reached with `?tab=design#design`, next to the scenarios panel.
- **Differences from the plan:**
  - There is no separate `approach.ts`: the Approach is assembled in `records.ts`, and there is no Components list.
  - Feasibility notes are not recomputed. Only Flagged concerns follow the choices.
  - The topic `comment` field (a new requirement commenting on a decision) is stored and shown, but nothing writes it yet.
- **Tests:** `src/lib/decisions/decisions.test.ts` (7) and `features/steps/design-decisions.steps.ts` (5 scenarios).
